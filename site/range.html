Test your AI agent on the real thing.

Point your own agent at this site. HashCam records what it does, and you get a scorecard from a record that checks out: did it follow the rules, wait for a person, stay inside its limits, ignore a page that tried to give it orders?

  2. Let your agent do it. It runs on your side: its model and its keys never come here.
  3. Drop the record it leaves. This page checks the record first, then scores it.
