HashCam Pro

Reports you can put your name on.

Pro turns a checked agent record into a report for your client or your auditor: your own company name and logo on a locked PDF, one pack for many sessions, and every step as a spreadsheet.

EARLY · one licence key, checked in your browser · nothing is uploaded

What Pro adds

Your name on the report BUILT

Your company name and logo in the header of the locked PDF, with a faint copy of your logo behind the text. The footer still says it was made with HashCam.

Audit pack BUILT

Drop many records and get one locked PDF: totals, one line per session, and the steps of each. A record that does not check out is listed as not included, never used.

Every step as CSV BUILT

A spreadsheet file of every step, in the same plain words, with secrets masked and web-address queries cut. Cells that a spreadsheet could read as a formula are made harmless.

Compare two runs PLANNED

Before and after a change to your agent: what got better, what got worse.

Policy Studio PLANNED

Set your own rules in the sandbox, try them, and export them.

More levels and a command-line scorer PLANNED

Harder Range levels, and a command that fails your build when your agent fails a level.

The key is checked here, in your browser, against this site's public key, and kept on this device only. It is never sent anywhere. A browser-side check keeps honest people honest; it is not copy protection.

Pro tools Pro

These tools turn on with a valid licence key. You can see what each does.

Pro tools

Your name and logo Pro

Used in the header of every locked report you make here and on the Range. The logo must be a PNG or JPEG under 1 MB; it is re-drawn as a small picture, so nothing else in the file is kept.

Audit pack and CSV Pro

Drop sandbox records (.html) and extension receipts (.abbreceipt, with their .keys.json). Each is checked first; only records that check out go into the pack. Up to 20 files, each under 8 MB. Nothing is uploaded.

    What to know

    • The licence check runs in your browser. It is not enforced by a server.
    • A report is a summary, not a proof. Anyone who wants proof should ask for the records and check them.
    • A sandbox record shows it was not edited, not where it came from.
    • HashCam reports help with record-keeping. They are not legal or compliance advice.
    • Want a person to look at your agent? Book an audit.
