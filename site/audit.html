Book an audit

A person looks at your agent's record.

If you build or run AI agents and want someone to read their records and rules and tell you what they see, you can book a manual review. It is a person's time, not a product.

Email to book About Pro

What a review covers

  • Your agent's receipts or sandbox records, checked with the HashCam verifier first.
  • Whether risky steps waited for a person, whether blocked steps stayed blocked, and whether the agent kept to its task.
  • Your rules: allowed sites, limits, and when a person has to approve.
  • A short written summary in plain words, with what to change next.

What it does not cover

  • It is not a guarantee that your agent is safe, and not legal or compliance advice.
  • It cannot see what a record does not contain, for example requests a page's own scripts sent.
  • It is not a penetration test of your systems.

How it works

  1. Email a few lines about your agent and what you want checked.
  2. You get a reply with what is needed and how long it takes.
  3. You send records, never passwords, keys or customer data.

This page has no form and no script. It only opens your email program.
